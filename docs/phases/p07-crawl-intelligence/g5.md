# G5 — is `page.changed` a useful scheduling signal?

Gate (audit §3.2, D-9): precision of `page.changed` ≥ 0.8 on ≥ 50 labelled
changed pairs from the seed set. The labels are held out: no P5/P7 rule is
tuned on them.

## 1. The question each label answers

> Would this observed change make a future revisit useful, because it shows
> meaningful new same-site content, new media, or materially relevant
> evolution of the page itself?

"The hash changed" is not the question. Every pair here changed its
normalized hash by construction.

## 2. Label schema (fixed before labelling, 2026-10-07)

| Label | Useful? | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `new_content_url` | yes | Revision B links to same-site content (a post, film or episode page) that is new on the site: a new release or newly published item, not one rotated in from the archive |
| `new_media` | yes | B references media (video/audio/manifest) that A did not |
| `material_content` | yes | The page's own content changed meaningfully: new download links or qualities, added episodes, a corrected or extended main text, a new film on a listing page shown as text |
| `template_churn` | no | Only rotating site-wide parts changed: "latest/related/popular posts" widgets, sidebars or menus showing already-existing archive items in another order or selection |
| `cosmetic_noise` | no | Counters, dates/times, view counts, random tokens, ad text, captcha/nonce text, whitespace-like differences |
| `metadata_only` | no | Only title/meta/date fields changed, with no visible content change worth revisiting |
| `spam_or_irrelevant` | no | The change is injected spam or content unrelated to the site's purpose (e.g. casino/SEO posts on a film site) |

When several apply, the **useful** label wins only if the useful part is
real. A widget that happens to show a new post counts as `new_content_url`.
An archive item rotated in is `template_churn`. The labeller also records a
`page_kind` (`home`, `listing`, `detail`, `other`) judged from the page
itself. Path depth is not used (audit §5.2).

## 3. Sample and procedure

- **Population:** the 325 `page.changed` transitions on seed domains
  (consecutive first sightings of a URL's revisions, M1 runs 1 and 2).
- **Stratified deterministic sample** (`g5_changes.py sample`): strata =
  domain × automatic new-URL flag. The sample takes every pair with the
  flag, every pair in strata of ≤ 15, and otherwise the first 15 by pair id
  (a SHA-256 order, which is arbitrary with respect to content). Precision
  is reported both on the sample and weighted by stratum population.
- **Evidence:** both raw snapshots from MinIO, re-extracted with P5. The
  recomputed normalized digest must equal the recorded one, so the diff
  shows exactly what `page.changed` saw. The labeller sees titles, the
  normalized-text diff, same-site links added/removed with anchors, and
  media added/removed. The automatic flag is hidden.
- **Leakage:** the derived signal ("B links to a same-site URL first seen
  at B") was defined in the reassessment (audit §5.2) **before** labelling,
  and it is not changed after. These labels must not be used to tune any
  P7 rule. A rule tuned on them would need a new, separate sample to be
  evaluated.

## 4. Result (2026-10-07): **G5 FAIL**

71 pairs labelled (`benchmarks/p7-intelligence/g5/labels.csv`, one-line
rationale each). Metrics: `results/g5-result.json`. All 142 snapshots
reproduced their recorded normalized digests.

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| precision of `page.changed`, sample | **0.070** (5/71) |
| precision, weighted by stratum population (325) | **0.015** |
| gate | ≥ 0.8 → **FAIL** |
| labels | spam_or_irrelevant 43 · template_churn 10 · cosmetic_noise 13 · new_content_url 5 · new_media 0 · material_content 0 |

| Domain | Pairs | Useful | What changed |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| isaimini.com.in | 25 | 0 | new casino/SEO posts rotated into "recent posts" widgets, on spam pages **and on old film pages**; 1 archive re-sample |
| tamilprint.com.in | 16 | 0 | the same spam widget swap on old film pages, plus a welcome-bar text toggle |
| moviesda.com.in | 12 | 0 | welcome-bar text ("X" → "Close Welcome Bar") and spam posts on the home page |
| moviesda.business | 13 | **5** | home/month/label listings showing newly published films. The 8 others are old archive pages whose month widget or pagination shifted |
| movflix.my | 4 | 0 | directory-index timestamps |
| tamilrockers2024.com | 1 | 0 | home list reordered existing 2025 posts |

By page kind: home 2/12 useful, listing 3/21, detail 0/31, other 0/7. **No
change on a detail page was useful.**

**The derived signal fails too.** "B links to a same-site URL first seen
at B" (defined before labelling, audit §5.2) scores precision 0.067 and
recall 0.2 in the sample (TP 1, FP 14, FN 4).

- Its false positives are new **spam** posts: they are new URLs, just not
  useful ones.
- Its false negatives occur because a new film is credited only to the
  first page the crawl happened to fetch after it appeared. The home page
  and the label/month listings of moviesda.business all showed it, but
  only one got the credit.

### 4.1 What this means for WHEN

1. Raw `page.changed` is **not** a usable scheduling signal on this
   population. Most of its events are site-wide widgets, and on two of the
   live domains those widgets carry SEO spam that the sites publish daily.
2. URL novelty alone is not usable either. It rewards spam publishing.
3. The useful changes were all on **hub pages** (home and listings) of a
   site that publishes films, and they consist of **new links to pages of
   the site's own content template** (`/YYYY/MM/<film>-movie-download-….html`).
   A defensible WHEN signal therefore needs WHAT first: a hub/detail
   distinction and the site's content URL template, so that "new links to
   content-template URLs" can be counted per hub. A URL template alone does
   not separate spam everywhere. On moviesda.business, films have their own
   template. On isaimini.com.in and tamilprint.com.in, film posts
   (`/lens-2017/`) and spam posts (`/wolf-run-slots-at-playrand/`) share
   WordPress's `/<slug>/`, so page features (a release year in the title or
   slug, link and title patterns) have to separate them.
4. WHEN stays **gated** (D-13). Its signal must be defined with the WHAT
   component and validated on a **new** labelled sample: these 71 labels
   are held out, and any rule that was informed by reading them cannot be
   scored on them.
